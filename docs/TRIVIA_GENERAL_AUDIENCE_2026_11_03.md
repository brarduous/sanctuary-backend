# General-audience Trivia, effective November 4, 2026

The owner confirmed that Easy → Medium → Hard should remain, but all three tiers must be much easier for ordinary players. The queued November 4 set included Naaman's requested gift and Lydia's city; those are outside the new audience standard.

Daily difficulty v3 keeps the four independently reviewed raw dimensions and weights familiarity twice. Every dimension must be 0 or 1. A value of 2 rejects the question at every tier: obscure recall, multiple relationships, multiple reasoning steps and fine distinctions are disallowed. The total is 0–5: Easy 0 (widely known recognition with plainly distinct alternatives), Medium 1–2 (common knowledge with credible alternatives or a helpful connection), Hard 3–5 (an accessible detail or one attainable connection within a recognizable story). Disagreement greater than two points rejects; fractional consensus rounds down. These remain editorial estimates, not measured audience success rates.

The writer receives the rubric, and all sources/angles now favor familiar scenes. Two independent factual/craft reviews, two separate blinded difficulty assessments, and two final set/rolling-week editors remain required. Evidence confidence remains >=95, craft >=7/8 with full fair challenge and no zero dimension. Version 1 and version 2 ratings and validators are preserved.

Installed mobile/web validators only accept v1/v2 numeric bands. The public API therefore encodes the new tier in the existing v2 label field (1/4/7) and explicitly marks it `difficultyEncoding: legacy-tier-compatibility`. That number is a compatibility code, not a remeasurement under v2. The exact independent v3 result is returned separately as `difficultyEstimate`; saved manuscripts and review records retain their actual v3 scores. This permits the difficulty change without requiring an app update. Legacy single-question clients still receive the Easy question through their existing projection.

`prepareAudienceSets.js` makes bounded model calls and saves all drafts, rejections, raw reviews and usage in a generation run. It never changes schedules. It can reuse saved factual/craft and raw difficulty assessments under the finalized v3 bands, with the source run recorded; it does not rerun a rejected manuscript seeking a favorable verdict. Each changed or new manuscript passes the full review path. Final reviews bind the exact manuscripts and current neighboring week, including earlier prepared replacements.

Two migrations add the v3 validator and a service-only reviewed replacement function. New daily publication from November 4 requires v3, so a stale worker cannot silently replenish the old difficulty. `discovery_replace_prepared_audience_set` accepts only a completed, fully reviewed preparation and the next seven future dates in America/New_York. It refuses a played set, stale weekly context, incomplete review, duplicate answer, invalid source/claim, non-v3 score or more than two replacements per date. The three new memberships switch in one transaction. Original content, attempts, membership audit and previously released copies remain available; today's questions and results are preserved. New unscheduled days use the existing worker and explicit revised-plan retry, retaining exhausted attempts.

Validation includes real PostgreSQL migration execution in isolated PGlite, ordinary and legacy API responses, protection of played dates, failed/stale replacement rollback, service-role isolation, original-history retention, and rejection of specialist recall at every tier. Hosted checks and production receipts are recorded separately after rollout.
